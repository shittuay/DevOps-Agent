Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

DevOps Agent (on the web at devopsagent.io and in the DevOps Agent iPhone app) is run by Urgency LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it. Questions: support@devopsagent.io.

  • We collect what we need to run the service: your account, your chats with the agent, and the cloud and tool connections you add.
  • To answer you, your messages and the results of the agent's actions are sent to AI model providers (listed below).
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Deleting your account deletes your data from our database.

1. What we collect

Your account

  • Email address, username, and any name you add.
  • Your password, stored only as a bcrypt hash.
  • Your two-factor sign-in settings (email codes or an authenticator app).
  • Your plan, credit balance, and settings and preferences.

Cloud accounts and tools you connect

  • AWS, Azure and Google Cloud connection details: for example an AWS role ARN and external ID, or access keys if you choose them.
  • Tokens and addresses for tools you connect in Settings › Integrations (such as GitHub, GitLab, Jenkins, PagerDuty, OpsGenie, Datadog, SonarQube and Nexus).
  • These are encrypted before we store them.

What you do in DevOps Agent

  • Your messages, the agent's replies, and the actions it runs with their results. Results can include details of your cloud resources, such as names, IDs, configuration and costs.
  • Resources we read from your connected accounts to show on the Dashboard and Map.
  • Requests waiting for your confirmation or a teammate's approval, and what happened to them.
  • Usage records: which actions ran, when, and the credits used.

Technical information

  • Your IP address and browser or device type, used for security, rate limits and fixing problems.
  • How you first found us: the referring website or campaign tag in the link you used (for example utm_source). It is saved with your account when you sign up.

Payments

Payments on the website are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. We keep your plan and subscription status and the Stripe reference for them. The iPhone app has no purchases.

In the iPhone app

  • If you allow notifications, a push token from Apple so we can tell you when an action needs your confirmation.
  • Before your first message, the app asks your permission to send your messages to the AI providers in section 3. Nothing is sent until you allow it, and you can withdraw it in Settings › Profile.
  • Face ID runs on your phone. We never receive your face data; the app only learns whether it succeeded.
  • The app doesn't access your contacts, photos, location or other apps.

2. How we use it

  • To run the service: answer your messages, act on the accounts you connect, and show your resources and costs.
  • To keep it safe: sign-in protection, account lockout, rate limits, and screening messages for abuse.
  • To email you: sign-in and security codes, confirmations, and account messages. We also send a short series of getting-started emails after you sign up and a weekly summary. Every one of those has an unsubscribe link.
  • To bill paid plans through Stripe.
  • To find and fix problems and improve the service.

We don't sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the service and its users from fraud or abuse.

4. How we protect it

  • Connections to DevOps Agent use HTTPS.
  • Cloud credentials and tool tokens are encrypted before they are stored (Fernet: AES-128 with an HMAC check).
  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes.
  • Two-factor sign-in is available, and risky actions on your cloud accounts wait for your confirmation (or a teammate's approval) before they run.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't promise yours will never be affected. Report security problems to security@devopsagent.io.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your account and everything tied to it (chats, connections, tokens, resources, requests, usage records and push tokens) from our database straight away, and cancel any paid subscription.

A few things are outside that deletion:

  • Information already sent to the companies in section 3 is kept under their own retention rules. Stripe, for example, keeps payment records it is required to keep by law.
  • Copies in server logs or database backups remain until those logs or backups are deleted.

6. Your choices

  • Delete your account: Settings › Profile › Delete account, on the website or in the iPhone app.
  • See and change your details: in Settings. You can disconnect cloud accounts and tools there at any time.
  • Keep a copy of a chat: use Export Chat in the profile menu on the chat page.
  • Stop marketing emails: use the unsubscribe link in any of them. Account and security emails still go out.
  • Stop sharing messages with AI providers (iPhone app): Settings › Profile › AI data sharing. The agent can't answer while it's withdrawn.
  • Turn off notifications: in your iPhone's Settings › Notifications › DevOps Agent.
  • Anything else, such as a copy of your data or a correction you can't make yourself: email support@devopsagent.io. Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights over your data; we'll help you use them.

7. Cookies and storage on your device

  • A sign-in session cookie, and a "remember me" cookie for up to 30 days if you tick that box.
  • A security token on forms, which protects against forged requests.
  • Your browser's local storage, for small preferences such as light or dark theme. In the iPhone app it also holds your push token.

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

8. Where your data is processed

We store your data in the United States. The companies in section 3 may process it in other countries.

9. Children

DevOps Agent is a tool for working on cloud infrastructure and isn't meant for children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new date.
